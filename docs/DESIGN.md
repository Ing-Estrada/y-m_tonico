## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the confluence of Colombian botanical heritage, high-performance dermatological care, and warm editorial luxury. Crafted for discerning skincare enthusiasts seeking mindful, clinical-grade depigmentation and natural radiance, the interface communicates calm authority, purity, and tactile sensory balance. 

Drawing from **Editorial Minimalism** and warm tactile wellness aesthetics, the visual experience avoids clinical sterile coldness. Instead, it invites the user into an organic sanctuary through expansive negative space, warm organic stone and linen undertones, disciplined typographic hierarchy, and softly grounded apothecary touches. Every viewport balances conversion clarity with serene storytelling.

## Colors

The palette derives inspiration from amber glass apothecary bottles, lush Andean botanicals, and sun-warmed river stones:

- **Marrón Profundo (`#3A2717`)**: The primary chromatic brand color. Represents the warmth of the amber glass formulation, artisanal apothecary roots, and premium prestige. Used for primary conversion triggers (e.g., "Añadir a la bolsa"), key value propositions, and luxury accents.
- **Verde Salvia Muy Claro (`#D1E5DD`)**: The calming secondary brand color. Informs container backgrounds, botanical highlight badges, routine pill steps, and delicate ingredient feature blocks.
- **Botanic Green (`#829762`)**: The tertiary accent pulled from native botanical leaves, reserved for certified organic seals, success states, and subtle botanical iconography.
- **Negro Carbón (`#191919`)**: The core neutral foundation. Ensures AAA reading contrast across navigation, headings, metadata, and high-density product specifications.
- **Cream Canvas (`#FAF9F6`) & Sand Surface (`#F5F3EF`)**: Auxiliary ambient backgrounds that prevent the clinical harshness of sterile white, lending an earthy editorial tactile softness.

Use pure white (`#FFFFFF`) sparingly as card elevations over cream surfaces to create natural, soft-layered lighting.

## Typography

The typographic pairing orchestrates a dialogue between classical elegance and utilitarian modern precision:

- **Playfair Display**: Drives emotional headlines, brand manifestos, ingredient spotlight cards, and testimonial titles. It delivers high-fashion prestige, evoking apothecary craftsmanship and sensory indulgence.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans**: Manages functional reading, body descriptions, nutrient tables, clinical percentages, and checkout UX. Its geometric construction and humanist touch provide flawless mobile readability and modern Scandinavian-style directness.
- **Editorial Sub-headers (`title-editorial`)**: Employs italic Playfair Display for poetic skincare descriptors (e.g., *"Tratamiento nocturno regenerador y unificador"*), elevating everyday functional e-commerce to self-care ritual status.
- **Micro-labels (`label-caps`)**: High letter-spacing (`0.14em`) in all-caps Plus Jakarta Sans is reserved for category tags, skin type classifications ("HOMBRE / MUJER"), and laboratory efficacy credentials.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses a flexible 12-column grid system bounded by a maximum container width of `1280px` for high-end balanced viewports, ensuring content never feels overstretched on ultrawide displays.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns, 16px (`gutter-mobile`) outer margins, 12px interior gutters. Product cards stack 1 to 2 columns with horizontal overflow scrolls for botanical routine steps.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px)**: 8 columns, 24px outer margins, 20px interior gutters.
- **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12 columns, 32px (`gutter-desktop`) outer margins, 24px gutters. Product detail pages leverage an asymmetrical 7:5 split (7 columns for high-resolution imagery/routine photography, 5 sticky columns for purchasing decisions and clinical results).

### Spacing Philosophy
Generous negative space (`space-3xl` and `space-4xl`) surrounds section headers and featured ingredient breakouts. This unhurried pacing creates a serene, premium spa-like browsing rhythm, resisting the cramped density of discount e-commerce platforms.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth avoids cold synthetic drop shadows, favoring atmospheric natural light reminiscent of morning sun filtering through botanical leaves:

- **Tonal Layers & Tinted Surfaces**: Rather than elevated white surfaces with heavy drops, structure is defined by layering `#FFFFFF` cards over `#FAF9F6` (Cream Canvas) and `#F5F3EF` (Sand Surface), framed with ultra-fine warm borders (`#E8E4DD` at 1px width).
- **Botanical Light Shadow**: For elevated interactive cards, purchase drawbars, and floating cart buttons:
  `box-shadow: 0 10px 30px -10px rgba(58, 39, 23, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -2px rgba(58, 39, 23, 0.03);`
  The shadow is warmly tinted with the primary amber-brown hue rather than dead grey, preserving organic warmth.
- **Active / Dropdown Overlays**: Modals and slide-in routine drawers utilize a warm blurred scrim:
  `background: rgba(25, 25, 25, 0.35); backdrop-filter: blur(8px);`

## Shapes

The geometric architecture relies on smooth, ergonomic radii mimicking smoothed river stones and cosmetic droppers:

- Standard elements (interactive inputs, smaller buttons, chip filters): `rounded-lg` (1rem / 16px).
- Product cards, imagery masks, hero containers, and wellness highlights: `rounded-2xl` (1.5rem / 24px) to `rounded-3xl` (2rem / 32px), creating a tactile, organic framing.
- Pills and Badges: Full pill radii (`9999px`) for application frequency tags, percentage claims (e.g., `"-40% en manchas"`), and category switches.

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Add to Bag / Comprar Ahora)**:
  - Background: `#3A2717` (Marrón Profundo)
  - Text: `#FFFFFF`, `label-button`, center-aligned with slight letter-spacing.
  - Shape: Rounded (`0.75rem` / 12px or `9999px` for pill CTA variations).
  - Hover: Subtle luminance lift to `#4E3520`, smooth transition `200ms ease`.
- **Secondary Action (Ver Rutina / Descubrir Ingredientes)**:
  - Background: Transparent with a 1.5px solid border in `#3A2717`.
  - Text: `#3A2717`, `label-button`.
  - Hover: Fill with `#F5F3EF`.
- **Botanical Soft Button**:
  - Background: `#D1E5DD` (Verde Salvia Muy Claro).
  - Text: `#191919`, `label-button`.
  - Hover: Fill with `#C4DDD3`.

### Cards
- **Product Card**:
  - Background: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`).
  - Border: 1px subtle line in `#E8E4DD`.
  - Radius: `rounded-xl` (16px) with an inner aspect-ratio `1:1` or `4:5` gallery frame set on `#F5F3EF` background.
  - Hover: Image cross-fade to model application shots, soft ambient brown shadow lift.
- **Wellness & Routine Feature Cards**:
  - Background: `#D1E5DD` (Sage) or `#FAF9F6` (Cream).
  - Typography: Playfair Display title with clean Plus Jakarta Sans step instructions.

### Badges & Chips
- **Efficacy & Claim Chips**:
  - Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padding `6px 14px`.
  - Background: `#D1E5DD` with text `#1E2421` (Dark Botanic).
- **Ingredient Highlight Pill**:
  - Bordered style: 1px border `#3A2717` at 20% opacity, background `#FFFFFF`, text `#3A2717`.

### Inputs & Form Elements
- **Text Inputs**:
  - Background: `#FAF9F6`.
  - Border: 1px solid `#E8E4DD`.
  - Focus state: 1.5px border `#3A2717`, no harsh neon halos.
  - Label: Floating `label-caps` in `#191919` with 60% opacity.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**:
  - Fill: Custom smooth circle/square with `#3A2717` active checkmarks.

### Special E-Commerce Modules

  - Clean horizontal strip utilizing Sand Surface (`#F5F3EF`), displaying three key claims in `label-caps` (e.g., *"REDUCE LA VISIBILIDAD DE MANCHAS • TESTEADO DERMATOLÓGICAMENTE • 100% BOTÁNICO"*).
- **Ritual Step Progress Bar**:
  - Minimalistic thin horizontal track in `#D1E5DD` with active step markers filled in `#3A2717`.
